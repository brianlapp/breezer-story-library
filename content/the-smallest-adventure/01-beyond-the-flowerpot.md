# Beyond the Flowerpot

Pip packed three crumbs, a button, and a map drawn on the back of a leaf.

“Where are you going?” asked the snail.

“Somewhere enormous.”

“The compost heap?”

“Further.”

The snail considered this. “Take a scarf.”

Pip took a scarf.

Beyond the flowerpot, the grass grew taller than stories. A beetle ferried him across a puddle in exchange for one crumb. A dandelion gave him directions, then changed its mind when the wind turned.

By lunchtime, Pip reached the fence.

Through a knot in the wood he could see another garden. It had flowers he'd never smelled, stones he'd never climbed, and a blue watering can as large as a mountain.

He sat on his button and ate the second crumb.

The world was so much bigger than he'd thought.

He felt very small.

Then a voice from the other side of the fence said, “Hello. Is there an adventure over there?”

Pip smiled and held up his last crumb.

“Yes,” he said. “And I've brought enough for two.”
